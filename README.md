## Physical Specifications:

**PCB OUTLINE:** 64 x 39mm 

**Mounting :** has 4 M3 screw holes  

**Microcontroller:** 1 Esp32-Wroom-32  

**Display :** One led Matrix +One TFT  

**Sensor:** USES MPU6050 to make it super reactiveeeee  .   
  

**USER:** CAN play many Mods in it 

## USER's instructions:  
  
>Let me guide you to how to use Pixelated Buddy
>  
>First of all you will be having 3 switches  
>To go to normal Led matrix mode just use SW 1 to go and frames of LED animations will play across  
>To go to super active LED matrix mode press SW 2 and when you do so when you move the keychain the Leds will flow on which direction you are tilting it  
>Press SW 3 to go to TFT mode (My favourite ).What you get here is 5 frames [Friendship,flirty,hungry,and Sleepy mode] Use SW1 to scroll through these modes[Remember you can press SW1 5 times then it goes to   >default mode . To exit mode 1 or 2 .Press SW3  
>Following this guide you will be good to gooo  
>Here's the Wokwi link if you wanna try it out = https://wokwi.com/projects/479750795108444161

## 3D part assembly :

>Place the bottom lid ,Screw the PCB with M3 screws ..Place the Led matrix in Front side and Secure it with glue  
>Connect TP4056 with Battery and connect it to the PCB then place the switches(with glue) and connect it .  
>Connect the TFT with PCB then place the Top lid and secure it with M3 screws  
>After following you should be good to goo
